10. Market Recovery Calculator

Purpose

Calculate how much a stock or index has recovered after a decline and determine how much gain is still required to return to the previous high.

Why it matters

Many traders incorrectly assume a 20% decline only requires a 20% gain to recover.

This tool demonstrates the true mathematics of drawdowns and recoveries.

Current Low (Optional)

Current Price

Professional Insight

• A 20% loss requires a 25% gain to recover.
• A 50% loss requires a 100% gain to recover.
• Large drawdowns require exponentially larger gains.
• Useful for evaluating whether a recovery is mature or still has significant distance to travel.
• Helps traders and investors maintain realistic recovery expectations.
