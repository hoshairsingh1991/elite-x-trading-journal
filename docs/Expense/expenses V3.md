# PURPOSE OF EXPENSES MODULE

The Expenses Module exists to track the business side of trading.

Trading performance and business performance are intentionally separated.

The Dashboard measures:

* Trading P&L
* Win Rate
* Profit Factor
* Drawdown
* Trading Performance

The Expenses Module measures:

* Business Expenses
* Trading Infrastructure Costs
* Subscription Costs
* Software Costs
* Data Feed Costs
* Funded Account Fees
* VPS Costs
* Education Costs
* Tax Deductible Expenses
* Net Business Profit

The goal is to answer:

"How much does my trading business actually cost to operate?"

and

"What is my true business profit after expenses?"

---

# CORE PHILOSOPHY

Trading P&L and Expenses must never be mixed.

Example:

Trading Profit

```text
$15,000
```

Expenses

```text
$2,500
```

Net Business Profit

```text
$12,500
```

This separation allows Elite X to function both as:

* Trading Journal
* Trading Business Management System

# UPCOMING RENEWALS ARCHITECTURE

Critical Doctrine

Upcoming Renewals are NOT historical analytics.

They are operational forecasting.

Therefore:

Upcoming Renewals must NEVER use:

```ts
filteredExpenses
```

They must ALWAYS use:

```ts
reportingExpenses
```

Reason:

Date filters answer:

"What happened?"

Upcoming Renewals answer:

"What is coming next?"

These are fundamentally different questions.

ExpensePage is the orchestration layer.

Analytics files own calculations.

Storage files own persistence.

UI components own presentation.

Date Range Picker owns selection UI.

ExpensePage owns filtering logic.

Historical widgets use filtered data.

Forecasting widgets use unfiltered reporting data.

This architecture must be preserved during all future development.
